✱ rapp-pets / 0.1

Alleles are to this ecosystem
what pets are to Adopt Me.

Adopt Me works because the pet is yours specifically: it hatched a certain way, it is a certain rarity, and everyone can see it. Rarity creates attachment; attachment creates a culture.

The naive copy fails, though, because Adopt Me's rarity is server-authoritative — a server rolls, records, and is trusted because it is the only writer. RAPP has no server. A rarity you store is worth nothing, because I can type {"rarity":"legendary"} into my own file. Any design where rareness is asserted is a design where rareness is free.

So the pet is not a record. The pet is your alleles drawn, and an allele is derived from the rappid tail — minted exactly once, never re-rolled. Nobody assigns it. Nobody can fake it. Anyone can verify it offline with a hash and no network.

✱ The nursery

Plant an organism and meet what it turned out to be. You do not choose it, and neither does anyone else — it falls out of the hash of an identity that can only be minted once.

✱ Kinship

Two organisms may carry the same allele, and that is a real relation between them — one you can verify without either one disclosing anything beyond a public tail. Paste two and see what they share.

✱ Fusion

Adopt Me's real engine is not its egg odds — it is the fusion sink. Four grown pets make a Neon, four Neons make a Mega, so a Mega costs sixteen pets. That one rule is what makes a duplicate worth keeping: without it most pulls are boring and get discarded; with it, every boring pull is an ingredient.

Same problem here — 3 in 4 organisms are common. So: four organisms fuse into one. fuse(tails) = sha256("rapp/1:fuse\n" + sorted(tails)) The result is an ordinary tail, so the child is an ordinary organism with the ordinary odds. Nothing is uplifted and no tier is inflated — fusing four mythics does not produce something better than mythic.

What the child does carry is its generation, and generation is arithmetic: gen 1 costs 4 minted identities, gen 2 costs 16, gen 3 costs 64. You cannot fake it, because you cannot produce the child without knowing four real mint-once tails — and anyone can recompute it offline to check. Depth is the scarce axis, and it is honest by construction.

What burning actually gets you. Each parent emits a nullifier — a spend-marker only its holder can compute, identical every time it is produced. On this device the burn is enforced: a spent organism cannot be spent again. Across devices it is detected, because spending one organism into two children publishes the same nullifier twice, and anyone holding both children holds the proof. No authority adjudicates that — the collision is the adjudication. What still needs an authority is ordering: deciding which of two conflicting fusions came first needs a clock somebody agrees on. So equivocation is caught, not arbitrated, and this page will not pretend to a verdict it cannot reach.

✱ What each trait does

traitbitsdrives the pet's…
coat8colourway and pattern
tempo8bob speed — how eagerly it acts
voice8species silhouette, ear lift, tail curl
glow16the aura — the one tiered slot

Only glow is tiered, because the spec's bands are defined over 16 bits. coat, tempo and voice are 8-bit variety: they colour the pet, they do not rank it. Calling an 8-bit value "mythic" would be inventing rarity the spec does not define.

Bands are exclusive, so the odds are the band widths, not cumulative thresholds. Rarity is honest work: to get a rare allele you mint identities until one lands, exactly like a vanity address. The scarcity is real because the electricity was real.

✱ What this must never become

  • Not a token. No transfer, no balance, no price. An allele is a property of an identity, and identity is mint-once — there is nothing to detach and sell. You have an allele; you never trade it.
  • Not a gate. An allele never affects what an organism may do. Capability comes from the mandate (Art. LVI), never from cosmetics. A mythic coat buys you a mythic coat.
  • Not private. Alleles are computed from the public tail — pure DOG, bones-side by construction. There is nothing here to leak.
  • Not inherited numerically. An organism planted from another is a new mint: fresh tail, fresh alleles. Heritable-by-copy would let one lucky mint be cloned into infinite rare children and the scarcity would evaporate in a day. What is inherited is descent — the chain of who planted whom.
  • Fusion is implemented — but it is not an attestation. Four organisms fuse into a child by pure derivation, so the child is an ordinary tail whose alleles come out of the ordinary bands: measured over 4,000 fusions, children land 74.5 / 19.3 / 5.9 / 0.35 / 0. Fusing four mythics does not produce something better than mythic — the scarce axis is generation, which is arithmetic. What is still not implemented is the co-signed attestation: four owners jointly asserting a shared trait needs §10 signatures and estate-owner authority, and shipping that before signing exists would be the same false-authority defect that retired rapp-frame-net.

✱ Verify any of it

Every pet on this page came out of this, and nothing else:

import hashlib
def allele(trait, tail, bits=16):
    h = hashlib.sha256(f"rapp/1:allele:{trait}\n{tail}".encode()).hexdigest()
    return int(h[:bits // 4], 16)

Same input, same answer, on any machine, forever. That is the whole design. allele.js and pets.js in this repo are byte-identical to the copies in rappdex, so the two cannot drift.

Spec: rapp-allele/1.0 in kody-w/rapp-mapp. Identity rules: RAPP/1 §6.2 (mint-once) and §5 (domain separation). Governance: RAPP/CONSTITUTION.md.
